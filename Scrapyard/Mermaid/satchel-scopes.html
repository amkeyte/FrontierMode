Satchel Scope Discovery

How scopes come and go for the three jig kinds. Level and player scopes are discovered from Forge load and login events. Mob scopes come from MobJig's own 20-tick reconcile poll over consumer interest sets, or from MobScope.getFor. Every new scope starts NEW and is promoted to LOADED on the next pulse.
