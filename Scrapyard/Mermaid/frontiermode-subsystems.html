FrontierMode Subsystems

Top-level map of FrontierMode's modules as built. Each module box lists its Satchel jigs: key, jig kind, side, capabilities, bundle and fixtures, and the Satchel events it handles. Double-ended dotted arrows to Satchel mean the module registers its jigs there and receives Satchel's events back. Solid arrows are real cross-module API calls, pointing from caller to callee. Plain dotted arrows go to the shared helpers. "Raw Forge" lines are the places that subscribe to Forge directly instead of going through Satchel.

Derived from (FrontierMode/src/main/java/com/arryn/frontiermode/)
  • FrontierMode, FrontierKeys, Rendering, Config, LogFilters
  • border/{BorderModule,BorderAPI}, border/common/{bundle,fixture,player,navigator}/*, border/server/{rules,commands}/*, border/client/render/level/*
  • boss/{BossModule,BossAPI,BossJigHandlers,BossMobJigHandlers,GuardianJigHandlers,BossPositionListeners,BossScaleEvents}, boss/common/{bundle,fixture}/*, boss/client/*, boss/server/*
  • bosstell/*, structure/*, feral/*, admin/*, effects/*, spawn/MobSpawns

Cross-module edges come from each package's com.arryn.frontiermode.* imports and the call sites behind them.
