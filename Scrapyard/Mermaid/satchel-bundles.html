Satchel Scope Engines and Bundles

How bundles and fixtures live inside a jig's ScopeEngine. Each jig gets its own engine, server- or client-flavoured. Bundles are created lazily on the first getOrCreate, through the coupler's access hooks. A server bundle becomes ACTIVE once it's hydrated. A client bundle that needs a capability waits, still CREATED, until its first parcel arrives. Only ACTIVE bundles tick.
