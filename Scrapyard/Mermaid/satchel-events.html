Satchel Events and Signals

Everything a consumer reacts to arrives on one SatchelEventBus per side. Lifecycle events come from Satchel's own dispatchers. Signals wrap a live Forge event that an ingress class re-posts, so a handler's veto lands on the real Forge event. The bus is shared by every jig and matches on the exact event class.
