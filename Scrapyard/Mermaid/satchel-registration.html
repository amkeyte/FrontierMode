Satchel JigConfig Registration

How a consumer's JigConfig becomes a running jig. Configs are collected in one static registry until the first foundation boots. Each side's booter then compiles, validates and installs the configs that apply to it, so a BOTH config yields two independent jigs and two engines, with its handlers installed on both sides' buses.
