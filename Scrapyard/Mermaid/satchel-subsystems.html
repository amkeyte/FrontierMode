Satchel Subsystems

Top-level map of Satchel's main subsystems as built. Raw Forge events enter through the ingress classes. Each logical side has its own LogicalFoundation, built by that side's booter from compiled JigConfigs. The per-tick pulse drives jigs, couplers and scope engines. Engines own the bundles and fixtures, persist them server-side and push them to the client as parcels. Everything a consumer module reacts to arrives on SatchelEventBus.
