## Textures and art

Original, by Arryn: the boss eye overlays, the frontier ring particle, and the item textures
diorite_shard and edge_stone.

Adapted from Minecraft's own assets: the Amethyst Compass frames (from the vanilla compass),
diorite_dust (recolored from the vanilla redstone dust texture), and diorite_striker (recolored
from the vanilla flint and steel texture). Mojang's artwork is not covered by this project's MIT
license; it remains subject to the Minecraft EULA (https://www.minecraft.net/eula) and Mojang's
usage guidelines.
